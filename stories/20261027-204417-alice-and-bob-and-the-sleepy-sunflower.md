Alice and Bob and the Sleepy Sunflower

Bob was Alice's best friend, and he was a clever gray cat. Alice lived in a yellow house beside a garden. The garden was full of humming bees and warm, sleepy flowers. Alice always kept her promises. Bob followed her everywhere. He always knew when her smile needed a little help.

One warm afternoon, Alice knelt in the dirt. She pressed a tiny sunflower seed into a pot.

"Bob, I promise we will watch it grow together," she said.

Bob tapped the pot with his paw. "Every day?"

"Every single day," said Alice.

Then gray clouds rolled across the sky. Alice peeked into the pot. The soil was still flat and brown.

She watered it. Then she watered it again.

"Nothing is happening," she sighed.

"It will grow soon," said Bob.

Alice spun around. "You don't know that!" she snapped. She turned away with a tight, sad frown.

Bob's ears drooped. For a moment, he looked ready to scurry under the porch. But he didn't. He sat down quietly beside the pot instead.

"I know you're worried," he said softly. "I'm here, even when the seed is slow."

Alice looked at the pot. She wanted it to grow so badly.

"I'm sorry I snapped," she whispered. She leaned against his soft fur. "Good friends stay close, sunshine or rain."

Bob's whiskers twitched. He had an idea. He tore a scrap of paper from her notebook. He wrote SPROUT GUARD in big, wobbly letters. Then he pressed one paw into a dab of mud and stamped it at the bottom.

He propped the sign against the pot. Then he stood up tall.

"Attention," he said in his deepest voice. "I am the Sprout Guard. Nothing gets past me."

He marched along the edge of the pot. He saluted. He stopped, sniffed the soil, and marched back again.

Alice giggled. "Guard, is the seed still there?"

"Yes, boss," said Bob, very serious. "Still there. Still sleepy. Still on duty."

Alice laughed so hard she held her tummy. Her sad feeling floated away like a bubble.

The next morning, Alice ran outside in her socks. A tiny green curl was poking up through the soil.

"Bob! Bob, come quick!"

Bob raced over, the Sprout Guard sign still tucked under one paw. His whiskers twitched. "It's growing!"

Alice grinned. "I promised to watch every day, and I did."

Bob puffed out his chest. "And I guarded it all night!"

They cheered together. "Hooray!" Alice hugged him tight. "Good friends stay close, sunshine or rain."

That evening, the garden grew quiet at last. Alice pulled a cozy blanket up to her chin. Bob curled up warm against her side.

The stars peeked out, one by one.

Alice's eyes felt heavy. Her breathing slowed.

"Good friends stay close," she whispered. "Sunshine or rain."

Bob purred, low and soft, like a little motor. Outside, the tiny sunflower leaf stretched in the moonlight, green and sleepy.

Alice drifted off, and Bob kept watch until morning.
