Alice and Bob Listen to the Night

A bedtime story

Alice loved bedtime best. Her cat, Bob, loved it too. Every night he curled up on her quilt and purred like a tiny engine.

"Ready for our walk?" Alice whispered.

Bob stretched. He yawned a big pink yawn. Then he hopped down from the bed.

"Look and listen, side by side," Alice said. That was their special saying.

They tiptoed out to the moonlit backyard. The grass felt cool and silver under their paws and toes.

Tonight the moon was big and round. Alice wanted to find the glowing fireflies before they went to sleep. Bob wanted to sit quietly by the garden gate and listen for the owl.

Alice raced across the grass. "Hurry, Bob! They're over here! Look at them blinking!"

Her words bounced across the yard. Bob stayed by the gate. His tail was curled tight around his paws.

Alice did not look back. She ran and ran. When she finally turned around, Bob was far behind. He was too far away to hear her happy words.

Alice stopped. Her toes wiggled in the wet grass. She looked back at the gate.

Bob sat very still. His ears pointed toward the fence.

Alice walked back slowly. Each step was soft. She sat down beside him.

"Shh," Bob whispered. His ears twitched toward the fence.

Alice held her breath. Then she heard it. A soft hoot from the old tree. *Hoo, hoo.*

The crickets chirped in the grass, too. *Chirp, chirp, chirp.*

"Oh," Alice said. "I didn't hear them before. I was too fast."

She rubbed Bob's soft ear. "I'm sorry I rushed. Will you show me what you hear?"

Bob's eyes glowed green in the moonlight. He purred. Then he pointed his nose at the bushes.

"Look," he whispered.

Tiny lights blinked on and off. They looked like little stars that had come down to play.

"Now I'll show you something," Alice said. She pointed at the grass. "Look at the moon's silver path. It goes right across the yard."

Bob padded onto the shiny path. He wiggled his whiskers. "Look and listen, side by side," he said.

"We each found something new," Alice giggled.

They padded back inside, yawning. Alice tucked Bob close under the quilt. He curled up in the warm spot by her tummy.

Outside, the owl hooted softly.

The crickets hummed their lullaby.

The moon sat high and quiet over the garden.

Alice closed her eyes. Bob's purr grew slow and small.

The two best friends drifted off, safe and happy, together.

The end

Goodnight.
